# Security Policy

We take the security of HivePostify and the people who use it seriously. Thank you for helping us keep the platform safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately. Do not open a public issue or post the details anywhere else.

1. Use the **Report a vulnerability** button on the Security tab of the affected repository, if it is available.
2. Otherwise, contact us through [hivepostify.com/support](https://hivepostify.com/support) and write "Security" at the start of your message.

Please include:

- A clear description of the issue and where it occurs
- The steps needed to reproduce it
- The impact you believe it has
- Your contact details, if you would like a reply

## Scope

This policy covers hivepostify.com and the repositories in this organization.

## Please do not

- Access, change or delete data that belongs to other users
- Run tests that disrupt the service, such as denial of service attacks
- Share a vulnerability publicly before we have had a chance to fix it

## What happens next

We review every report, confirm the issue, and work on a fix. If you would like to be credited for your report, tell us how you would like your name to appear.
