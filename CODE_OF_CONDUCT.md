# Code of Conduct

We want HivePostify to be a respectful and welcoming place for everyone who takes part, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient with other people
- Give and accept feedback politely
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Do not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be removed or blocked, and repeated or serious cases may lead to a permanent ban from the repositories. To report a concern, contact us through [hivepostify.com/support](https://hivepostify.com/support).

This Code of Conduct is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
